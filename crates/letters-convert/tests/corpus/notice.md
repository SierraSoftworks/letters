# Notice of rates

Interest compounds as $(1 + r)^n$, or in full:

$$
A = P \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}
$$

The table and Figure 1 show the change.

| Year | Rate |
| --- | --: |
| 2026 | 4.0 % |
| 2027 | **4.5 %** |
